Security Claims

ComplianceFlow separates what was observed from what can safely be claimed. These definitions are part of the product's trust model.

StateMeaningAllowed claim
OBSERVEDProvider-backed evidence exists with lineage.The provider reported the observed state.
VERIFIEDEvidence passes deterministic verification requirements.The observed control state is verified.
POTENTIALA relationship/path is plausible but not fully provider-verified.Describe as potential; do not present as confirmed exposure.
INCONCLUSIVEEvidence is missing, stale or insufficient.State uncertainty; do not claim success or failure beyond evidence.
REMEDIATEDAn authorized action was applied and entered verification.Describe remediation lifecycle state; do not skip verification.
PROVENFresh independent evidence plus completed deterministic reanalysis demonstrates measurable security reduction.Claim the specific verified security effect.

Exposure paths

A path such as Internet → Workload → IAM → S3 is only VERIFIED when every required relationship has provider-backed provenance and the nodes have sufficient evidence. Otherwise it remains POTENTIAL.

Compromise

ComplianceFlow does not infer confirmed compromise from an exposure path, severity score, AI reasoning, or correlation. Confirmed compromise is a separate evidentiary state and is never manufactured by the analyst.

Remediation proof

Proof is a chain: authorized action → real cloud mutation → fresh provider observation → completed reanalysis → deterministic path/risk comparison. Equal or increased risk does not qualify as a security reduction.

AI analyst

security-analyst-v7 identifies the semantic analyst contract. It governs grounding and allowed explanations; it is not a substitute for deterministic security authority.
