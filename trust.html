Trust Center

Security claims should be provable.

ComplianceFlow is built around durable evidence, deterministic security analysis and explicit remediation verification. This page describes the product's trust model and the claims it deliberately refuses to make without sufficient evidence.

Durable authority

PostgreSQL is authoritative for execution state, findings, evidence, exposure paths and audit events. Redis/BullMQ is dispatch infrastructure, not security truth.

Multi-cloud

Provider-backed analysis spans AWS, Azure, GCP, DigitalOcean and Hetzner. Observations remain tied to their originating evidence.

Verification

Remediation verification requires fresh provider evidence. Missing, stale or incomplete evidence is inconclusive—not success.

Remediation safety

Authority is policy-driven. Safe automation and higher-impact actions have distinct approval requirements before execution.

Evidence lineage

Security claims carry evidence and scan lineage. Reanalysis uses fresh evidence rather than recycling the triggering observation.

AI boundaries

The AI analyst explains deterministic findings, relationships, risk and proof state. It cannot invent relationships, override deterministic truth or infer compromise from correlation.

Security result vocabulary

Observed

A provider-backed observation exists with identifiable lineage.

Verified

The observation satisfies deterministic verification requirements, including freshness and integrity where applicable.

Potential

A relationship or exposure path is plausible but lacks enough provider-backed evidence to be presented as verified.

Inconclusive

Available evidence cannot safely establish the requested state. Uncertainty is not converted into success.

Remediated

An authorized remediation was applied and subsequently evaluated through the verification lifecycle.

Proven

A security-effect claim is supported by independent fresh evidence and completed deterministic reanalysis showing measurable reduction.

AI disclosure

Analyst contract versions such as security-analyst-v7 identify the semantic rules used for explanations. Findings, evidence, graph state and remediation proof remain deterministic and auditable.

Claims we do not make

  • Correlation alone does not prove compromise.
  • An AI response does not override provider evidence.
  • A remediation API success response does not itself prove the cloud state changed.
  • An incomplete reanalysis cannot claim that an exposure path disappeared.
